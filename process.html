Before the appointment

A little preparation goes a long way.

A ready property helps the shoot move efficiently and keeps the finished images focused on the home.

01

Clear the surfaces

Reduce countertop clutter, personal items, and anything that distracts from the room.

02

Turn on the lights

Replace burned-out bulbs and open blinds or curtains where natural light helps the space.

03

Ready each room

Make beds, straighten furniture, hide cords, and complete cleaning before the appointment.

04

Plan for access

Confirm entry details and communicate any occupied-property instructions before arrival.
